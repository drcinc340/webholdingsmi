About Web Holdings & Firm Leadership

Founded in Southeast Michigan to bridge the gap between complex custodial registries and the rightful beneficiaries, estates, and institutions owed dormant proceeds.

Our Approach

Accountable, Local Michigan Representation

Across federal judicial districts, insurance carrier consolidations, and municipal treasury ledgers, rightful payees frequently go unnotified when an older residence is sold, a family member passes away, or a business updates its billing address. Recovering those funds requires exact docket cross-referencing, statutory lineage affidavits, U.S. Attorney proof of service, and notarized execution.

Web Holdings LLC operates on a straightforward principle: our firm performs the audit discovery upfront, advances all administrative and notary costs, meets Southeast Michigan clients in person at established local financial institutions, and earns a fee solely when the client's disbursement clears.

In-person notary execution coordinated across Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, and Washtenaw Counties, with certified priority and remote notary support nationwide.
