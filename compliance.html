Statutory Framework & Right to Independent Claim

Web Holdings LLC is an independent private audit and claim-preparation firm. Pursuant to federal and state transparency principles, prospective clients are advised that government custodians maintain public portals where rightful owners may search and file claims directly without fee if the holding agency is independently identified:

28 U.S.C. § 2042

United States Courts Unclaimed Funds

Governs custodial deposits held at the U.S. Treasury for uncashed federal judicial distributions, requiring formal petition, claimant identity proof, and notice to the United States Attorney.

Official U.S. Courts Overview (USCourts.gov) →

U.S. Dept. of Housing & Urban Development

Governs unearned FHA mortgage insurance premium refunds and distributive share disbursements, including authorized Third-Party Tracer filings via Form HUD-27050-B.

Unclaimed Life Insurance & Statutory Registries

Zero Third-Party Advertising or Data Resale

This website loads zero third-party advertising trackers, analytics beacons, or external script networks. Client contact details submitted through our inquiry portal are never sold, rented, or shared with external marketing organizations.

Encrypted Record Storage & Limited Scope

Client inquiries are stored under authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption. Formal Powers of Attorney executed during engagement are strictly limited to prosecuting the single identified disbursement and confer zero authority over any personal bank, brokerage, or estate account.
