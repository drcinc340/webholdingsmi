Governance & Standards

Client Protection & Engagement Protocol

Every Web Holdings engagement adheres to strict procedural safeguards designed to protect client privacy and eliminate financial risk.

01 / CONTINGENCY ONLY

Zero Out-of-Pocket Expense

Clients never pay retainers, research fees, notary charges, or filing expenses. Compensation is earned solely as an agreed percentage after funds are successfully recovered.

02 / PRIVACY PROTOCOL

No Telephone Credential Requests

Our office never requests Social Security numbers, dates of birth, online banking credentials, or payment card details during telephone outreach or online verification.

03 / IN-PERSON EXECUTION

Local Banking & Notary Signing

Across Southeast Michigan, Managing Partners Adriano Vagnini and Josh Dinverno coordinate notarization in person at your preferred neighborhood bank branch or licensed notary office.

04 / DIRECT DISBURSEMENT

Payable to Rightful Owner

Official U.S. Treasury electronic transfers and insurance custodian checks are issued directly to the verified claimant, beneficiary, or corporate entity whenever permitted by statute.

Client Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

How did Web Holdings identify an uncollected balance associated with my name or family? +
Our firm audits public federal court registries, HUD mortgage insurance ledgers, municipal check registers, and statutory unclaimed insurance databases where original disbursements went uncollected due to an older mailing address, corporate name change, or estate transition.
When is the holding institution or case docket disclosed? +
Because Web Holdings performs all audit research and skip-tracing prior to engagement with zero upfront fee, we confirm your historical city/street record and exact dollar balance first, and provide full written custodial and docket disclosure immediately upon execution of our 1-page Contingency Engagement Agreement.
What if a claim is not approved or no funds are recovered? +
You owe nothing. All audit, genealogical lineage, notary, and filing expenses are borne entirely by Web Holdings unless and until your disbursement clears.
May rightful owners search public government registries independently? +
Yes. Individuals and businesses always retain the statutory right to search government portals directly at no fee if they independently identify the holding jurisdiction and wish to prepare the required motions, affidavits, and U.S. Attorney notices themselves.
