Questions Skeptical Claimants Ask Us Every Day

Why don't you tell me which court or insurance agency holds the check before I sign Page 1? +
Because we spend our own money and audit engineering to locate dormant checks and trace relocated families while charging $0.00 upfront. If we named the exact court docket before signing, people would bypass us after we did all the discovery work. Instead, we prove it is 100% real upfront by confirming your exact old address and down-to-the-penny dollar amount, meet you at your own bank notary, and hand you the full government/carrier disclosure the instant Page 1 is signed.
How did you get my cell phone number? +
Government unclaimed-funds dockets and insurance escheatment ledgers list the rightful payee's name and their old mailing address from years ago. Because the check went uncashed at that old address, licensed public-record address-history databases are used strictly to match the old street address to the rightful owner's current phone number so you can actually be notified.
What if the government or insurer denies the claim — could I ever owe you money? +
Never — not one cent. Section 1 of our written agreement explicitly states that if no funds are recovered for any reason, you owe $0.00 and never reimburse us for notary, postage, or filing costs.
