Division I • Life Insurance & Annuities

Dormant Life Insurance Policies & Survivor Death Benefits

Life insurance proceeds frequently go uncollected when policyholders pass away without surviving family members locating physical policy contracts, or when historical underwriters merge, change names, or convert from mutual to stock ownership.

Web Holdings audits statutory unclaimed insurance registries (including accounts governed by MCL 567.256a), reconstructs beneficiary lineage where primary beneficiaries are also deceased, and prepares Small-Estate Affidavits (such as Michigan Form PC 598 where eligible) and carrier release filings.

Division II • 28 U.S.C. § 2042

U.S. Federal Judicial & Treasury Custodial Funds

Uncashed dividend, wage, escrow, and creditor distribution checks issued in federal proceedings are deposited into the U.S. Treasury unclaimed funds registry pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 2042. Release requires formal court application, identity and address chain verification, vendor registration (AO-213P), and certified notice to the United States Attorney.

Our office prepares the complete motion packet, coordinates notarization, serves the appropriate U.S. Attorney's Office, and tracks judicial order entry through direct U.S. Treasury electronic disbursement.

Division III • HUD & County Surplus

HUD / FHA Distributive Shares & Foreclosure Surpluses

Former homeowners who paid off or refinanced FHA-insured mortgages are often owed unearned premium or distributive share refunds held by the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. Similarly, county treasurers and Sheriff's Sale ledgers hold overbid surplus equity (MCL 600.3252) prior to state escheatment.

As a third-party tracer, Web Holdings prepares official HUD-27050-B and county claim documentation with zero upfront cost to former homeowners or their estates.

Healthcare, Law Firm & Corporate Disbursement Recovery

Hospitals, physician groups, law firms, auto finance companies, and commercial vendors routinely accumulate uncashed judicial registry distributions and municipal vendor warrants tied to legacy lockboxes, former billing addresses, or acquired subsidiaries.
